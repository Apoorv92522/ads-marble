Our Story

ADS Marbles & Articles —
Surat's Marble Masters

Established in 1999 in Surat, Gujarat, ADS Marbles & Articles has built a distinguished reputation over 25+ years as a premier manufacturer of marble temples, statues, crafts and artwork.

Under the guidance of Mr. Arpit Shah (Proprietor), our skilled artisans use high-quality marble and advanced techniques — delivering crack-resistant, beautiful marble pieces to clients across India.

  • Crack-resistant, high-strength marble construction
  • 100% custom designs tailored to your space & vision
  • Experienced team with advanced manufacturing infrastructure
  • Fair pricing · timely delivery · client-first approach
  • Custom packaging & pan-India road delivery
